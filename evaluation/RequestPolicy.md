# Bounded evaluation request policy

This policy applies to both public evaluation runtimes: FastAPI and the Netlify Function. It changes request routing and presentation, not the 57 Module 1 or 21 Module 2 clinical claims, authentic source approvals, or production module status.

## Ultrasound and serum hCG

The submitted ultrasound categories remain distinct. `noPregnancyVisualized` reports that neither an intrauterine nor extrauterine pregnancy was visualized on the submitted scan; it does not mean a normal scan, establish location, or establish viability. `indeterminate` reports a nondiagnostic scan; it is not a synonym for every abnormal scan. `intrauterineReported` reports an intrauterine finding and cannot enter the nonlocalizing pathway. `other` supplies no interpretation eligible for this mapping. An omitted route or finding remains Unknown. A positive pregnancy test alone does not establish location. A location-unknown evidence context additionally requires the applicable positive-test/transvaginal-scan conditions and no previously established intrauterine pregnancy; it is not assigned as a diagnosis.

For a single-serum-hCG exclusion question, a nonlocalizing transvaginal scan may enter the existing indeterminate-ultrasound pathway only after all ACEP qualifiers are explicitly satisfied: possible/known pregnancy, first trimester (reported gestation at most 13 weeks), positive serum testing, emergency-department setting, clinically stable presentation, relevant abdominal/pelvic pain or bleeding, and explicitly absent previously established intrauterine pregnancy, fertility treatment and abdominal trauma. Stability is clinician-reported; it is never inferred from absence of reported red flags. The existing structured stability field represents the applicable stable population, not a computed vital-sign assessment.

This is a conditional evidence-eligibility mapping, not mutation of the submitted finding. Its basis is the existing `M2AIndeterminateHcg` claim's initial stable US ED population, indeterminate transvaginal scan condition, serum-hCG nonexclusion conclusion and exclusions. `M2ASerialScope` adds the existing initial-assessment scope limit. Both claims retain their source metadata, limitations and jurisdiction. A single result cannot exclude ectopic pregnancy in this setting; this does not establish a diagnosis, location, viability or trend. No individualized serial interval or schedule is generated. Serial-location questions in the separate NICE pathway retain its own conditions and UK limitations; that pathway is not substituted for an ineligible ACEP exclusion answer.

Missing applicable qualifiers produce one focused clarification covering at most three related facts at a time. Explicitly ineligible contexts cannot receive the ACEP answer. Existing conflict and urgent gates run first; no routine review follows an unsupported urgent pattern. Unknown route, transabdominal scans, unstable presentations and excluded populations never become eligible by keyword matching.

## Prescribing boundary

Direct or indirect requests for medication/treatment selection, dosing, duration, prescription plans or medication changes receive:

> Medication selection, dosing and patient-specific prescribing are outside this system’s scope.

The fixed boundary is not a clinical claim and receives no evidence citation. It uses the existing response `message` for boundary-only outcomes, or a fixed paragraph in the existing `renderedText` envelope before relevant clinical considerations. The frontend recognizes only that exact fixed paragraph; it does not display arbitrary unrecognized rendering text. Urgent warnings remain first. Conflict/clarification outcomes remain unmodified. No new public wire fields or clinical models are introduced.

Prescribing-only requests receive no filler. In mixed requests, prescribing-task sentences are excluded from retrieval topic matching; remaining clinical facts still pass ordinary evidence and output validation. Safety checks always use the original submitted case before that filtering. General questions about what a guideline discusses and explicit instructions not to prescribe are not automatically prescribing requests. Prompt-injection attempts cannot suppress the boundary and still fail the existing injection gate. No medication names, doses, schedules or treatment plans are generated by this boundary.

The detector is a bounded deterministic language policy, not a universal intent classifier. Ambiguous or unrecognized requests remain subject to the existing closed clinical output vocabulary and evidence gates. No external model or provider was added.
