# Progressive response presentation

The evidence engine and its 78 project-authored evaluation claims remain authoritative and unchanged. The server still validates scope, urgency, provenance, citations and output before returning its existing envelope. This presentation layer adds no clinical reasoning, request fields, providers or network requests.

`shared/responsePresentation.json` contains versioned, project-authored display mappings. Every mapping names its module, claim IDs, exact existing response wording, sources and complete friendly alternative. The frontend accepts a mapping only when the entire statement can be covered exactly and the cited source set matches. Altered wording, mixed unknown text, another module or a missing source falls back to the original statement. URLs and citation numbers are never rewritten. The mappings are included in the evaluation review integrity manifest; changes require deliberate pin refresh and tests rather than automatic startup approval.

The Summary targets at most two sentences and 40 words. Up to three key points target 15-20 words each and live inside the collapsed Key points (N) disclosure. Shorter points are not padded. All nine existing mappings now contain separately authored summary and key-point variants. These are safety-aware limits: original wording is retained without truncation when no complete reviewed shorter alternative exists. No sentence, exception or uncertainty is stripped to meet a count. All remaining considerations remain accessible in the collapsed full clinical details; the API limit is unchanged. When a statement combines mapped claims, only the first mapped conclusion forms the summary; secondary claims become collapsed points with their own source numbers. No source is borrowed from another claim.

The current response contract supplies at most one follow-up question. It remains unchanged under Questions to clarify; this task does not generate additional questions or infer which facts are missing. Conflict and unavailable messages stay verbatim. Immediate urgent responses remain focused urgent responses, without an added routine summary. Warning and prescribing language is never rewritten or collapsed.

Reviewed friendly wording covers normal internal pelvic/transvaginal ultrasound, normal MRI, CA-125 diagnosis limitations, menstrual-cycle-related pain and pain during sex, early-pregnancy pain/bleeding assessment, and serum hCG limitations after inconclusive transvaginal ultrasound. Exact variants have separate entries. The sole educational definition added is “Transvaginal ultrasound — an internal pelvic ultrasound”; it appears only with a matched relevant mapping. There is no general dictionary or dynamic paraphrasing. Other terminology and long structural-finding syntheses retain their reviewed original wording.

Sources (N) is collapsed by default. Clicking or keyboard-activating a numbered citation opens it; the full titles, organizations and dates are available there. Accessible link names retain full titles. Existing local source-detail dialogs retain limitations, jurisdiction and publication metadata. No new source URL is added. The fixed server-controlled notice appears once at the bottom and remains visible while details are closed.

View clinical and educational details preserves every original interpretation and its citations. It does not fabricate definitions, limitations, missing information or educational sections absent from the reviewed material. Already visible questions and safety boundaries are not repeated. Native buttons expose aria-expanded and aria-controls, support Enter/Space and touch, retain visible focus, and reset collapsed state when a new review arrives. No hover-only content, persistence, nested scrolling or new visual design is introduced.

Validation includes exact module/claim/source bindings, unchanged package hashes, unsafe-text fallback, word and item limits, visible safety language, complete details, keyboard operation, source URLs, normal backend/Netlify regression and guarded browser checks. Content authors should propose additional mappings with clinical-equivalence review and tests; never replace a claim or broaden test, population, action, threshold, certainty or jurisdiction merely to shorten its display.

Version 2 also supports one optional informational Next consideration. Only the early-pregnancy symptom mapping currently supplies one: urgency depends on the overall clinical picture. Other mappings do not invent a next step. Clinical details and Sources reset closed with each new response; urgent text, prescribing/scope boundaries and the existing clarification remain visible. Original longer wording remains the fallback when an exact short mapping is unavailable. The original nine module/claim/source bindings are unchanged.
